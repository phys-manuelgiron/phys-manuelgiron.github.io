Experimental photonics May 2026 — present

Finding order in
scattered light.

I investigate random lasing and optical emission in biological scattering media at Florida Polytechnic University, including seashell-derived particles in Rhodamine B–glycerol.

My contribution Sample preparation, pulsed-laser experiments, and Python workflows for peak fitting, linewidth analysis, and spectral comparisons. I also connect spectrometers, energy meters, motorized stages, and Arduino controls for automated measurements.

  • Spectroscopy
  • Python
  • Data acquisition

02Numerical optics · 2026

Designing light-selective
photonic glazing.

A 33-layer TiO₂/CaF₂ structure with a Fabry–Perot cavity for passive solar heat management. I implemented transfer-matrix modeling with wavelength-dependent refractive indices across 400–1600 nm.

03Scientific computing · 2026

Building models.
Checking the assumptions.

For the Mathematical Contest in Modeling, I developed a lunar-colony logistics model for a proposed 100,000-person settlement, evaluating transportation capacity, resource constraints, and operational losses.

In numerical methods coursework, I implemented a one-dimensional finite-element heat-equation solver with implicit-Euler time stepping and checked convergence against a manufactured solution.

  • Sensitivity analysis
  • Finite elements
  • Numerical validation

03 / A little context

Curiosity, with
a method.

I’m a physics undergraduate drawn to the connection between what we can measure and the theories that explain it.

My experience so far is in optics and photonics: preparing samples, collecting spectra, building analysis workflows, and testing numerical models. I’m especially interested in carrying those experimental and computational skills into high energy physics, while deepening my understanding of quantum field theory.

I’m looking for summer research stays and industry opportunities where I can contribute to scientific computing, instrumentation, or experimental analysis—and keep learning from the people around me.

↳

Beyond the lab, I’ve taught SAT students and helped found Florida Poly’s Phi Sigma Pi chapter, where I serve as vice president. I speak Spanish and English, and I’m beginning to learn Japanese.
